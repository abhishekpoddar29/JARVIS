Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of workouts do you offer?
We offer a wide range of workouts including strength training, cardio, yoga, HIIT, and more, suitable for all fitness levels.
Can I customize my meal plan?
Yes, our Premium plan includes custom meal plans designed by nutritionists based on your dietary preferences and goals.
Is there a free trial available?
Currently, we do not offer a free trial, but our Basic plan is a great way to get started with minimal commitment.
How do I track my progress?
Our platform includes an intuitive dashboard where you can log your workouts, track measurements, and visualize your progress over time.
What if I need to cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. There are no hidden fees or long-term contracts.
